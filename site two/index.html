FAQs

We've got the answers

Is SikaVest regulated by the Bank of Ghana?

Yes, SikaVest operates in partnership with BoG-licensed asset management firms and utilizes Tier-1 banking infrastructure to ensure complete regulatory compliance and security of funds.

How to contact my dedicated advisor?

Pro and Enterprise clients have direct access to their advisors via the in-app secure chat, or through priority phone support available Monday to Friday, 8AM to 5PM GMT.

How do I integrate Mobile Money?

Simply navigate to the "Integrations" tab in your dashboard, select MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, or AT Money, and follow the API binding instructions for seamless corporate sweeps.

What are the minimum deposit requirements?

Our corporate starter plans require a minimum treasury deposit of GHS 10,000 to access fractionalized T-Bills and fixed-income assets.

Can I withdraw funds instantly?

Liquid assets can be withdrawn to your linked corporate bank account within 2-4 hours. Fixed-term instruments are subject to their respective maturity dates.
